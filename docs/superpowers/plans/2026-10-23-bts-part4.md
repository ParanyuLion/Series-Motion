# BTS Zombie Series, Part 4 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Produce `out/bts_part4.mp4`, Part 4 ("ฝ่าไปหัวขบวน") of the BTS zombie series, from the approved Thai script.

**Architecture:** Same as Parts 1 to 3: composition `Bts4` in `src/bts4/` and `scripts/bts4/`, timing from the narration audio through `src/bts4/manifest.json` (every cue derived from the line timings, nothing hard-coded), a synthesised sound bed, reuse of `src/bts/` components. Part 4 moves through the train, so it is a chain of short scenes joined by a travelling camera and swish cross-fades: car 4 (mirrored, as in Part 3), car 3 (items), car 2 (the fight, a normal non-mirrored car, survivors walk LEFT toward the front), car 1 (the sliding door), the cab door. `Passenger` gets backward-compatible `item` and `swing` props for the improvised weapons.

**Tech Stack:** Remotion 4, React, TypeScript, SVG, Python 3.13 (numpy, scipy), TTS via the Vertex AI backend of `scripts/tts_gemini.py` (`TTS_BACKEND=vertex`, location global, project from `Remotion/.env`), ffmpeg, node 22.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/bts-part4-script-draft.md` (approved by the user on 2026-10-23, "อนุมัติ"), on `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-bts-zombie-series-design.md`. Rules: `series-animation/SKILL.md` and `references/`. Templates: `src/bts3/`, `scripts/bts3/`, `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-23-bts-part3.md`.

## Global Constraints

- 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264 plus AAC; total 50 to 72 s; story ends by 67 s; manifest constants `LEAD 6`, `TAIL 8`, `HOOK_TAIL 50`, `HOOK_LEAD 21`, outro 135 frames.
- The ten narration lines are copied verbatim from the draft (each at most 56 Thai characters without spaces). Voice Charon, `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview`, speed 1.1, post-speed 1.2, Vertex backend. On an API-key, auth or quota error STOP and tell the user; do not switch model or voice.
- Implied violence only: zombies stagger and tumble (cartoon), no blood, no gore; ป้าสมจิตร's end is a silhouette and a sound; the near-bite shows unbroken skin. Zombies never jump or run (0.25 to 0.5 px per frame); the survivors may walk fast.
- Many short beats with zooms and cut-ins during the fight, never one static wide shot; never an empty or static picture; the sound bed is energetic (risers, pulse, stingers, swishes), no dead silence.
- Safe area: subtitles Prompt 500, size 52, `left 60, right 160, bottom 340`, two lines; figures above screen y 1414; name tags inside screen x 130 to 880; nothing floats; face-on close-up at the emotional beat; characters who talk face each other.
- Loudness -20 to -14 LUFS; bed `<Audio volume={0.5}>`. Never print, log or commit secrets. Commit after each task and push to `main`.

## Review Focus

- The fight reads as fighting: three distinct actions (umbrella jab, extinguisher cloud, bag swing), zombies stagger and fall, nobody overlaps a zombie except inside a hit, the group visibly advances.
- Camera/scene transitions are smooth (no hard cuts without a swish or a fade); camera keys in order (guard).
- ป้าสมจิตร's end: silhouette and sound only; afterwards three survivors remain (you, พี่นุ่น, ไมค์); the later scenes never draw her or the boy as a survivor.
- The near-bite: the strap is caught, skin unbroken, the clock flash does not read as "you" being infected.
- Clock "น้องเต้ย" reaches 0:00 exactly at the rise; Part 1 to Part 3 output unchanged (rig props are optional).
