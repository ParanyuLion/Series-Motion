# BTS Zombie Series, Part 1 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Produce `out/bts_part1.mp4`, Part 1 ("ประตูปิด") of the BTS zombie series, from the approved Thai script.

**Architecture:** A new composition `Bts1` in the existing Remotion project (`E:\Coding\animation\Remotion`), in new folders `src/bts/` and `scripts/bts/` so the island code is untouched. Timing comes from the narration audio lengths through a generated `src/bts/manifest.json` (same pattern as the island Part 1: cues are frame numbers derived from audio, nothing hard-coded). All pictures are SVG drawn by code; the sound bed is synthesised with numpy.

**Tech Stack:** Remotion 4, React, TypeScript, SVG, Python 3.13 (numpy, scipy), Gemini TTS via `Remotion/.env`, ffmpeg, node 22.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-bts-zombie-series-design.md`. The approved Part 1 script is `docs/superpowers/specs/bts-part1-script-draft.md`; its ten narration lines must be copied verbatim. Project rules and lessons live in `series-animation/SKILL.md` and its `references/` (read `visual-style.md` and `pipeline.md` first).

## Global Constraints

- Video 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264 plus AAC, one MP4 per part. (island series spec; same here)
- About 60 s of story, a few seconds over is fine, plus a 4.5 s outro on top; total between 55 s and 72 s. The opening hook animation is inside the 60 s. (spec: Goals)
- 3 to 5 significant events in the part; Part 1 has five: (a) hook, (b) first bite, (c) the clock and the panic, (d) retreat to the end of the car, (e) the driver's announcement. (spec: Goals; script draft)
- Second-person narration "คุณ", one narrator, Gemini voice **Charon** proposed; no other character voices. Narration budget about 7.5 Thai characters per second excluding spaces; the script has 428. (spec: Voice)
- Look: bright morning interior, **yellow seats**, the city passing outside with parallax. The student ("you"): white long-sleeve shirt, green tie, black trousers, backpack, lanyard. Zombies: slow, head tilted, arms forward, pale green-grey skin, little blood. (spec: Look and feel; the user's choices)
- No graphic gore: bites and deaths are implied by silhouette, sound and cuts. (spec: Non-goals)
- Train: 4 cars joined by an open walkway; "you" board at car 3 and retreat toward the rear (car 4); the cab is at the front of car 1. The train does not stop at stations. (spec: World rules, Assistant's calls)
- The bite clock is 60 s and is a story device: shown as an on-screen countdown, not real time. (spec: Assistant's calls)
- Safe area: burned-in Thai subtitles, Prompt 500, size 52, two lines at most, 60 px left, 160 px right, 340 px bottom; figures above the subtitle band (feet above about y 1414 on screen). Nothing may float; the picture is never empty. (island rules in `series-animation/references/visual-style.md`)
- Loudness between -20 and -14 LUFS. Music stays under the narration (bed `<Audio volume={0.5}>`, ducked).
- `GEMINI_API_KEY` is read only from `Remotion/.env`; never print it, log it or commit it. Gemini free tier is about 10 requests a day per model; do not burn requests blindly (cache, one model per run).
- Commit after each task and push to `main` of Series-Motion. `out/`, `.env`, `node_modules/` are git-ignored.

## Review Focus

- Countdown numbers that do not match the bite and the turn (the number 0:00 shown before the bite or after she stands up). Expected: the bitten woman stands up exactly when the countdown reaches 0:00, derived from cues. Pinned in Task 4 and Task 5.
- A Thai subtitle wrapping to three lines or leaving the safe area. Expected: two lines inside the safe area. Pinned in Task 5.
- Violence staged too explicitly. Expected: the bite is a silhouette plus a sound, no blood, no visible wound beyond a small red mark. Pinned in Task 2 (the bite is an effect that cannot draw gore).
- The train map hard to read at phone size (labels overlapping, car 3 not obviously where "you" are). Expected: four labels never overlap and the "you" marker is highlighted. Pinned in Task 4.
- Narration audio longer than the budget after the real TTS run, or a Gemini failure mid-run. Expected: the verify step stops with a clear message; real files are replaced only if every line succeeded. Pinned in Task 1.

## File Structure

| `scripts/bts/part1.narration.json` | The 10 approved narration lines (strings). |

- Cue keys, all global frames: `hookCut` (180), `lineStart0..9`, `lineEnd0..9`, `doorsClose` (= `lineStart0` + 20), `manTurns` (= `lineStart2`), `biteAt` (= `lineStart3` + 20), `countdownStart` (= `biteAt` + 10), `womanRises` (= `lineEnd4`), `stationPass` (= `lineStart5` + 30), `retreatStart` (= `lineStart6`), `mapIn` (= `lineStart6` + 40), `mapOut` (= `lineEnd7`), `announceStart` (= `lineStart8`), `cabOpens` (= `lineStart9` + 40).
