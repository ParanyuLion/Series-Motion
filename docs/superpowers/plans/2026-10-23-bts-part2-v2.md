# BTS Zombie Series, Part 2 v2 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Rebuild `out/bts_part2.mp4` from the revised, approved Part 2 script (the driver warns the zombies, a zombie falls onto him and bites a fingertip).

**Architecture:** Same as the first Part 2 build (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-22-bts-part2.md`): composition `Bts2` in `src/bts2/`, scripts in `scripts/bts2/`, timing from the narration audio through `src/bts2/manifest.json`, sound bed synthesised by `scripts/bts2/bed.py`. This plan changes the narration lines 0 to 5, the cue set, the driver and zombie choreography, one new close-up (the driver's face), the title card, and the bed events around the bite.

**Tech Stack:** Remotion 4, React, TypeScript, SVG, Python 3.13 (numpy, scipy), Gemini TTS via `Remotion/.env`, ffmpeg, node 22.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/bts-part2-script-draft-v2.md` (approved by the user on 2026-10-23: "อนุมัติ"), which sits on `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-bts-zombie-series-design.md`. Rules: `series-animation/SKILL.md` and `references/` (especially "Tight pacing"). Old plan for the shared parts: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-22-bts-part2.md`.

## Global Constraints

- 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264 plus AAC; total between 50 and 72 s; story ends by 67 s; manifest constants `LEAD 6`, `TAIL 8`, `HOOK_TAIL 50`.
- Narration lines are copied verbatim from the v2 draft (10 lines, each at most 56 Thai characters without spaces). Voice Charon, `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview`, speed 1.1, post-speed 1.2. Lines 6 to 9 are unchanged from the first build, so they come from the cache; lines 0 to 5 need new requests. If the API answers "API key not valid" or the quota is gone, STOP and tell the user; do not switch model or voice.
- No graphic gore: the bite is a silhouette plus a sound plus a small red mark. Zombies stay slow: no jump; the zombie falls forward onto the driver.
- Safe area and subtitle rules as before (left 60, right 160, bottom 340, two lines). Name tags inside screen x 130 to 880. Zombies never overlap the living outside the bite silhouette. Face-on close-ups at emotional beats. The picture is never empty. Sound keeps its energy (risers, pulse, stingers); the only silence is line 8.
- `GEMINI_API_KEY` only from `Remotion/.env`; never print it. Commit after each task and push to `main`.

## Review Focus

- The clock reaching 0:00 exactly when ลุงประเสริฐ rises (derived from cues).
- The falling zombie overlapping the driver for more than the silhouette flash; the driver's feet or the fallen zombie inside the subtitle band.
- Name tags clipped at the screen edge; the new face close-up under the subtitle (white shirt/uniform behind white text).
- Camera keys out of order after the cues change (the guard in `camera.ts` throws).
- A Gemini failure mid-run replacing files (the script is atomic).
