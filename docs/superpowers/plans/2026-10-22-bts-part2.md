# BTS Zombie Series, Part 2 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Produce `out/bts_part2.mp4`, Part 2 ("คนขับ") of the BTS zombie series, from the approved Thai script.

**Architecture:** A new composition `Bts2` in `E:\Coding\animation\Remotion`, in new folders `src/bts2/` and `scripts/bts2/` so Part 1 stays untouched. It reuses the Part 1 building blocks from `src/bts/` by import (`Passenger`, `PASSENGERS`, `TrainInterior`, `FLOOR_Y`, `Countdown`, `StationBoard`, `ZombieFace`, `ANCHOR`). Timing comes from the narration audio through a generated `src/bts2/manifest.json` (cues are frame numbers derived from audio; nothing hard-coded), exactly like `scripts/bts/manifest.mjs`. The sound bed is synthesised with numpy.

**Tech Stack:** Remotion 4, React, TypeScript, SVG, Python 3.13 (numpy, scipy), Gemini TTS via `Remotion/.env`, ffmpeg, node 22.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-bts-zombie-series-design.md`. The approved Part 2 script is `docs/superpowers/specs/bts-part2-script-draft.md` (the user approved it in chat on 2026-10-22: "พาท2 ผมโอเคละ"); its ten narration lines must be copied verbatim. Project rules and lessons live in `series-animation/SKILL.md` and its `references/` (read `visual-style.md`, `pipeline.md`, `feedback-log.md` and `series-bible-bts.md` first). The Part 1 plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-22-bts-part1.md` and the code in `src/bts/` and `scripts/bts/` are the template.

## Global Constraints

- Video 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264 plus AAC, one MP4 per part.
- About 60 s of story plus a 4.5 s outro; total between 55 s and 72 s; story ends by 67 s. The spoken hook is inside the 60 s.
- Every part opens with a spoken hook "จะเกิดอะไรขึ้น ถ้าคุณ..." that names the zombies: narration line 0 is "จะเกิดอะไรขึ้น ถ้าคุณเห็นซอมบี้กัดคนขับ แล้วไม่มีใครเชื่อ".
- Five events: (a) the driver comes out, (b) the bite and "ไม่เป็นไร", (c) "you" see the bite and nobody believes you, (d) ลุงประเสริฐ turns, (e) the train does not stop.
- Second-person narration, one narrator, Gemini voice **Charon**, model `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview`, speed 1.1, then `--post-speed` as needed (Part 1 used 1.2). The 3.8 model reads the style prompt aloud: do not use it.
- Look: bright morning car, **yellow seats**, city with parallax. Student "you": white long-sleeve shirt, green tie, black trousers, backpack. Zombies: slow, tilted head, arms forward, pale green-grey, little blood.
- No graphic gore: the bite is a silhouette plus a sound, no blood, only a small red mark.
- Safe area: burned-in Thai subtitles, Prompt 500, size 52, two lines at most (56 Thai characters without spaces at most per line), `left: 60, right: 160, bottom: 340`; figures above the subtitle band (feet above about y 1414). Nothing floats; the picture is never empty or static; face-on close-up at emotional beats; no jarring cuts.
- Loudness between -20 and -14 LUFS; music under the narration (bed `<Audio volume={0.5}>`, ducked).
- `GEMINI_API_KEY` only from `Remotion/.env`; never print, log or commit it. Free tier is about 10 requests per day per model: use the per-line cache, one model per run, `--post-speed` (free) for pace; fall back to the 2.5 model for single lines, then to `scripts/island/tts_edge.py` as a provisional voice and say so.
- Zombies move slowly (0.25 to 0.5 px per frame) and never touch the living; bitten people are bitten off-screen or in silhouette.
- Commit after each task and push to `main` of Series-Motion. `out/`, `.env`, `node_modules/` are git-ignored.

## Review Focus

- The driver's countdown not matching the turn: ลุงประเสริฐ must rise exactly when the clock reads 0:00, and the clock must not be mistaken for the driver turning (the driver's clock is a viewer-only graphic over the cab door; the uncle's rising is the payoff). Derived from cues; pinned in Task 2.
- Continuity with Part 1: ไมค์, ป้าสมจิตร and ลุงประเสริฐ fled into car 4 at the end of Part 1; Part 2 must show where they are (see the staging ruling below) and the driver is healthy until the bite. Pinned in Task 2 (stills).
- Zombies touching or overlapping the living or the driver outside the bite silhouette. Pinned in Task 2 (stills at the approach and the bite).
- A Thai subtitle wrapping to three lines or leaving the safe area; the quoted word "ไม่เป็นไร" in line 3. Pinned in Task 1 (subtitle check) and Task 2.
- Narration longer than budget or a Gemini failure mid-run. Pinned in Task 1 (`verify.mjs` stops with a clear message; files replaced only if all lines succeeded).

## Staging rulings (the plan's calls; the executor may refine, ledger any change)

- The scene is car 3 only, camera anchor (540, 820), world coordinates as in Part 1 (`FLOOR_Y` 1300, car width about 1080 plus margins, the walkway on the right edge at about x 1500 to 1700).
- The driver comes from the **left** (the front of the train) through the open end of the car; he walks in with both hands raised; he leaves the same way after the bite and the cab door closes off-screen (a short cab insert at line 4, not a new set).
- The two Part 1 zombies (`man1`, `woman1`) start the part at about x 700 and x 820 shambling right, slowly. They bite the driver at about x 600 (silhouette). Afterwards they keep shambling toward the group but stop short of it at the end.
- Survivors huddle at the rear end of car 3, near the walkway: `you` x 1180, `nun` 1290, `nueng` 1400 (seated, shaking), `mike` 1500, `aunt` 1600 (seated). `uncle` sits at x 1260 on the bench (seated, pale, sweating, hand under his shirt). Ruling: in Part 1 they ran through the walkway; in Part 2 they have come back to the walkway mouth because the next car is just as crowded; say nothing about it in narration. Ledger it as a ruling.
- Camera never leaves car 3's right two thirds except for the first shot of the driver walking in (x about 200 to 600) and the bite.
