# BTS Zombie Series, Part 3 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Produce `out/bts_part3.mp4`, Part 3 ("รถไม่จอด") of the BTS zombie series, from the approved Thai script.

**Architecture:** Same as Parts 1 and 2: composition `Bts3` in `src/bts3/` and `scripts/bts3/`, timing from the narration audio through `src/bts3/manifest.json` (all cues derived from the line timings), a synthesised sound bed, reuse of `src/bts/` components. Part 3 is set in car 4, whose walkway to car 3 is at the left end (the zombies come from the front), so `TrainInterior` gets a backward-compatible `mirror` prop; `TrainMap` gets a backward-compatible fix so its labels follow the "you" marker's car.

**Tech Stack:** Remotion 4, React, TypeScript, SVG, Python 3.13 (numpy, scipy), TTS via the Vertex AI backend of `scripts/tts_gemini.py` (`TTS_BACKEND=vertex`, location global, project from `Remotion/.env`), ffmpeg, node 22.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/bts-part3-script-draft.md` (approved by the user on 2026-10-23, "ok"), on `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-bts-zombie-series-design.md`. Rules: `series-animation/SKILL.md` and `references/` (pipeline, visual-style, series-bible-bts, feedback-log). Templates: `src/bts2/`, `scripts/bts2/`, `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-23-bts-part2-v2.md`.

## Global Constraints

- 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264 plus AAC; total 50 to 72 s; story ends by 67 s; manifest constants `LEAD 6`, `TAIL 8`, `HOOK_TAIL 50`.
- The ten narration lines are copied verbatim from the draft (each at most 56 Thai characters without spaces). Voice Charon, `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview`, speed 1.1, post-speed 1.2, Vertex backend. On an API-key, auth or quota error STOP and tell the user; do not switch model or voice.
- No graphic gore (the bite is a silhouette, a sound and a small red mark); zombies never jump or run (0.25 to 0.5 px per frame) and never overlap the living outside the bite flash; hooks and titles do not give away events; tight pacing and an energetic sound bed (risers, pulse, stingers, swishes), no dead silence.
- Safe area: subtitles Prompt 500, size 52, `left 60, right 160, bottom 340`, two lines; figures and anything else above the subtitle band (screen y 1414); name tags inside screen x 130 to 880; nothing floats; face-on close-up at the emotional beat.
- Loudness -20 to -14 LUFS; bed `<Audio volume={0.5}>`. Never print, log or commit secrets. Commit after each task and push to `main`.

## Review Focus

- The shove chain: ไมค์ shoves พี่นุ่น, she staggers into น้องเต้ย (to her left, nearest the zombies), he falls toward the zombie; nobody overlaps outside the silhouette flash; the fallen boy's body does not run through the zombie.
- The clock "น้องเต้ย" starts at the bite and is still running at the end (open cliffhanger); the survivor count card reads 5 then 4.
- Station boards, the map card and the count card legible and not overlapping the subtitles or each other; name tags inside the safe area.
- Mirror prop: station and car texts not mirrored; Part 1 and Part 2 output unchanged (render one still of each).
- Camera keys in order (guard in `camera.ts`).
