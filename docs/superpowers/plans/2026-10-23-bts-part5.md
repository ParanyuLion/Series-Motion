# BTS Zombie Series, Part 5 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Produce `out/bts_part5.mp4`, Part 5 ("ห้องคนขับ", the finale) of the BTS zombie series, from the approved Thai script.

**Architecture:** Same as Parts 1 to 4: composition `Bts5` in `src/bts5/` and `scripts/bts5/`, timing from the narration audio through `src/bts5/manifest.json` (every cue derived from the line timings), a synthesised sound bed, reuse of `src/bts/` components and of `src/bts4/camera.ts` (`makeCamera`). Scenes: one main scene (the cab room at the left, car 1 at the right, joined by the cab door, which is the only door), a brake close-up overlay, a platform scene, a face close-up of "you".

**Tech Stack:** Remotion 4, React, TypeScript, SVG, Python 3.13 (numpy, scipy), TTS via the Vertex AI backend of `scripts/tts_gemini.py`, ffmpeg, node 22.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/bts-part5-script-draft.md` (approved by the user on 2026-10-23), on `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-bts-zombie-series-design.md`. Rules: `series-animation/SKILL.md` and `references/`. Templates: `src/bts4/`, `scripts/bts4/`, `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-23-bts-part4.md`.

## Global Constraints

- 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264 plus AAC; total 50 to 72 s; story ends by 67 s; the ten narration lines verbatim from the draft (at most 56 Thai characters without spaces); Charon, `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview`, speed 1.1, post-speed 1.2, Vertex backend; stop and tell the user on any key, auth or quota error.
- No door between cars (open walkway); the cab door and the cab's side door are the only doors. Implied violence only (silhouette and sound); zombies never jump or run (0.25 to 0.5 px per frame); the survivors are three: you, พี่นุ่น, ไมค์.
- Safe area: subtitles Prompt 500, size 52, `left 60, right 160, bottom 340`, two lines; figures above screen y 1414; tags inside x 130 to 880; nothing floats; no hard cuts without a cross-fade; energetic sound, no dead silence.
- Loudness -20 to -14 LUFS; bed `<Audio volume={0.5}>`. Never print or commit secrets. Commit after each task and push to `main`.

## Review Focus

- The drag and the push-out read clearly and are not gory; the driver is a recognisable zombie (uniform, cap).
- The horde never overlaps the survivors; the cab door closes before it reaches them.
- The platform ending shows relief and danger together; figures stay above the subtitle band; the sign text is readable.
- Part 1 to 4 output unchanged.
