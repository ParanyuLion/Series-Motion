## The rule
In this village everything has a shadow except the people. Trees, houses, the well, a goat, a bucket all cast shadows in the harsh sun; the villagers cast none. "You" are the only person with a shadow. The villagers are kind and polite, speak in unison when they greet, and never stand in the shade of anything.

## The truth (told only at the end)
The village's shadows are not lost: they were taken by the great tree in the middle of the village. Its shadow never moves with the sun, and if you look long enough its dark is made of thousands of thin silhouettes, the shadows of everyone who ever came. The villagers each sold their shadow to the tree to stay as they are (they do not age, they do not tire) and the tree wants a new shadow every season. The first people to arrive were its first meals. The head of the village is the oldest shadowless person; he knew from the first minute; the kindness is the way they keep a newcomer near the tree until the right evening. The twist of the rule: the tree can only take a shadow that a light has made. In total darkness "you" have no shadow, so darkness is the only safe place, and the villagers' lanterns are the weapon. At the end "you" escape in the dark, but at home the first lamp you light shows a bare wall: the tree already had it. The last scene: "you" at the edge of the village, shadowless, saying the sentence the head said in Part 1 to the next arrival, with a shadow of their own on the ground.

## Motif (like the dusk bell)
Noon: a wooden clapper struck once on the tree at the exact moment the sun is highest and every shadow is smallest, and every villager looks down at their own feet together for one second (the habit of checking that there is nothing there). It returns in every part.

## Look
The opposite of series 4: bleached high noon. Overexposed pale gold sky, white-hot ground, deep blue-black hard shadows under objects, heat shimmer, dust, a few cicada-like ticks. Everything with a long ink shadow except the people, so the missing ones stand out at once. As the days pass the sun lowers and the shadows lengthen, the story's clock. One accent colour: the village's red paper charms on the doors. The tree's shadow is the one place that is cold and blue. At dusk the village lights its lanterns (warm orange) and "your" shadow becomes the darkest thing in the frame. ## The four parts (about 1:25 each)
1. **"เงาของคุณ"**. Hook: "จะเกิดอะไรขึ้น ถ้าคุณเป็นคนเดียวในหมู่บ้านที่ยังมีเงา / และทุกคนในนั้นสังเกตเห็นแล้ว". You step off the truck in the white noon. The first thing you see is a child under a tree: the tree has a shadow, the child does not. The head greets you in a warm unison with the villagers, then looks at your feet and says only "เงาสวยจังเลย". Tour: villagers walk in the open and step around every patch of shade; a woman moves her washing out of the shadow of the house. The noon clapper: everybody looks down together. You hide your shadow by standing under the head's hat, by standing where the sun is overhead. End: you notice your shadow is slightly longer than at the clapper; the sun has moved; everyone has stopped working and is watching it grow. Teaser "ตอนต่อไป: ห้ามเดินในที่ร่ม".
2. **"ห้ามยืนในที่ร่ม"** (the rule that rules). Hook for a cold viewer. The supper at the long table in full sun at noon (they eat in the glare, no one shades their eyes); a stranger's kindness: they lay a cloth over "your" shoulders to make you comfortable, you see your shadow on the table, a hand quietly covers it with a plate. You try the shade of the granary to hide: the doorway is empty of people and the shade inside is cold; you feel something look at you; you leave. The villagers explain "ใต้ร่มมันไม่ปลอดภัยสำหรับคนมีเงา" (true, said as kindness). You see the great tree for the first time from far away: its shadow does not move with the sun. End: your shadow, which you had at your feet, points the wrong way for one second, toward the tree.
3. **"ต้นไม้ที่มีเงาเยอะเกินไป"**. Hook. The long afternoon: the head "invites you to the tree for the welcome". The tree up close: a cold blue shadow pool in the white ground; at its edge, thin dark shapes lean as if listening; a child's shape waves. The head, still gentle, tells the story of the first traveller as a shadow-puppet scene on the village wall (as in blind Part 2, but with a lantern: the puppet loses its shadow in the story). The mother holds "your" hand tightly: "อย่ากลัวนะ มันไม่เจ็บ". The lantern is lit while the sun is still up (a staged dusk). You break away. End: the dusk clapper; every villager lights a lantern together.
4. **"ทางออกที่ไม่มีแสง"** (the finale). Hook. A night chase by lantern light: your shadow stretches across the village walls, huge, and the lanterns follow it. You put out the one lamp you have and walk in the dark (no shadow, no light, the villagers lose you; their lanterns are the only things you can see, like stars). You reach the road, the truck, the city, home at dawn. At home, first light on the wall: nothing there. The last scene: the head's sentence "เงาสวยจังเลย" spoken by you to a new arrival, who has a shadow; the closing card "จบแล้ว... แต่ยังมีคนใหม่มาเรื่อยๆ" / "ขอบคุณที่ดูจนจบ" (as the blind-village finale).
