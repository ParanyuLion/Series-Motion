# "หมู่บ้านที่ทุกคนตาบอด": story plan for all five parts (for the user's approval)

Companion to `2026-10-23-blind-village-series-design.md` (premise, truth, rules, format). Written 2026-10-23. The user wants all parts planned together because every part links to the others. Marked **(user)** = decided by the user; **(call)** = the assistant's proposal, to be accepted or changed. The title "หมู่บ้านที่ทุกคนตาบอด" and part 3 are accepted by the user.

## Timeline (call)

Part 1: morning. Part 2: afternoon into dusk. Part 3: that night and the next day. Part 4: the next dawn to dusk, in the forest. Part 5: the night road, then home at dawn. Five days of fear compressed into about three.

## The threads that run through every part

Each thread is planted early, grows, and pays off in part 5.

1. **The shirt pattern (a snake with a human head) (user).** Part 1: everybody wears it; the face on the pattern is blank and calm. Part 2: it is on the cups, the well rim and the table cloth. Part 3: it is woven into the huts' mats and carved on the posts; "you" are given a shirt with the pattern to wear (call). Part 4: the same shape is carved on tree trunks, and the real snakes hang in that shape. Part 5: the face on your own shirt is your face (call).
2. **The eyes.** Villagers have clouded white eyes; "you" have pupils (the secret is visible to the viewer in every shot). Part 3: a test with something only a seeing person would avoid. Part 4: "you" must keep the eyes still while the snakes look. Part 5: your pupils narrow to a slit at the very end (call).
3. **The man who could see = the village head (user).** The head is the first person who could see; he vanished in the forest, was turned into a snake with a human head, and now wears a human shape as the blind village head. Part 2: he tells the story of the seeing man in the third person (the viewer only understands later that it is his own). Part 3: "you" find a patternless shirt and a row of scratches counting days in the roof of a hut (his old shirt and his count, kept by him). Part 4: the snakes in the trees are the village's other hidden watchers; none of them shows his face yet. Part 5: the reveal.
4. **The head knows from the first minute (user).** He is a snake in disguise, so he knew that "you" can see from the start. Part 1: "มีคนมาที่นี่อีกแล้วหรอ" (said as the arrival of one more, said by the one who has seen the others) and "ตาบอดใช่มั้ย" (a test he already knows the answer to). Part 2: "ถ้าคุณมองเห็น เราคงลำบากกันน่าดู" (the story of himself, with two meanings). Part 3: his stick moves the stool before you reach it (he is keeping you in the game, not helping you). Part 4: the snake that lowers its face to your eyes is calm like him. Part 5: the last kind sentence at the forest edge and the final talk, both of them as snakes.
5. **The evening bell (call).** Part 1: once at dusk a bell rings and every villager stops and turns the same way, toward the forest, for a few seconds. Part 2: the head says it is "to say thank you for the day". Part 3: "you" see what they face (a line of trees; something moves). Part 4: the snakes in the trees are what they were facing. Part 5: at home, at dusk, "you" hear the bell and your head turns by itself toward the window (the last sound and picture).
6. **No reflections, no names (call).** Part 1: no mirror, no still water; nobody asks for your name, they call you "คนใหม่". Part 3: a bowl of water is always covered. Part 5: at home the first mirror shows the change; someone calls your name and you do not turn at first.
7. **Care as a threat (user).** Part 2: the villagers say, with warmth, that they once made a seeing newcomer blind so that he would not vanish. Parts 3 and 4: every kind gesture is read twice by the viewer. Part 5: the head's kindness and the snakes' delight are the same gesture.
8. **Memory of arriving (user: "you" remember nothing).** Part 1: you wake with no memory. Part 3: a flash, a feeling of being carried through leaves. Part 5: the memory returns as a clear picture of snakes carrying you and laying you down gently, and the pleased hiss of the trees as you leave the forest (the snakes are delighted with "you", user).

## Part 1: "ตื่นขึ้นมา"

- **Hook:** จะเกิดอะไรขึ้น ถ้าคุณตื่นมาในหมู่บ้านที่ทุกคนตาบอด แต่มีคุณคนเดียวที่มองเห็น
- **Beats:** wake with no memory (a woven mat, forest sounds); a figure walks up with a stick tapping, the head: "มีคนมาที่นี่อีกแล้วหรอ"; "คุณตาบอดใช่ไหม?" ; you answer "ใช่" (face-on close-up, the key beat); the walk through the village (huts, a well, a long table); everybody in the same pattern shirt; the pattern's calm blank face; no mirrors; the dusk bell and everyone turning toward the forest together (the first wrong thing); the head says "ยินดีต้อนรับ".
- **Plants:** threads 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 8.
- **Cliffhanger:** the villagers, still facing the forest, say together "ยินดีต้อนรับ" without turning to you, while the pattern on every shirt seems to look at you. Hook for part 2: the head promises to tell "you" why they are careful.

## Part 2: "คนที่มองเห็น"

- **Hook:** จะเกิดอะไรขึ้น ถ้าคนที่เคยมองเห็นในหมู่บ้านนี้หายไปแล้ว
- **Beats:** the communal table at dusk, the cups with the pattern; the head's story of the seeing man and the foraging trip, told in the third person (shown as a soft shadow-puppet like flashback so no gore; it is his own story); the line "ถ้าคุณมองเห็น เราคงลำบากกันน่าดู" (two meanings); the villagers mention, warmly, that they once made a seeing newcomer blind so that he would not vanish; the bell and the turn toward the forest.
- **Plants:** threads 3, 4, 5, 7.
- **Cliffhanger:** the head asks everyone to be glad that "you" cannot see; you realise what would happen if they found out. Hook for part 3: the first night.

## Part 3: "คืนแรก"

- **Hook:** จะเกิดอะไรขึ้น ถ้าคุณต้องแกล้งตาบอดต่อหน้าคนที่ไม่เคยหลับตา
- **Beats:** the hut at night; an old woman feeds you the same food as everyone and watches your hand; **the test:** a villager "accidentally" leaves a stool in your path, the head's stick moves it a second before you reach it (thread 4: he keeps you in the game); next day, tasks (carrying water, weaving, with the pattern); you are given a pattern shirt; hidden in the roof you find a patternless shirt and scratches on a post counting days (the head's, thread 3); a flash of being carried (thread 8); at the evening bell you see what moves in the line of trees (thread 5); you decide to leave.
- **Plants:** threads 1 to 8 all touched.
- **Cliffhanger:** the trees move again, in the shape of the pattern. Hook for part 4: you walk into the forest.

## Part 4: "ป่า"

- **Hook:** จะเกิดอะไรขึ้น ถ้าป่าที่คุณกำลังหนีเข้าไป มีอะไรมองคุณอยู่ทุกต้น
- **Beats:** dawn, you slip away; the forest is too quiet; foraging marks; you find a patternless shoe; the first snake with a human head above you, still; then a crowd on every branch, all turned toward you; none of them shows a face you know, and the viewer feels something familiar in how calmly they watch (thread 4); you recall the head's story and keep your eyes dead and your steps hesitant like a blind person; you walk under them (the long, silent crossing); one lowers its head to your face and looks straight into your eyes; you do not blink.
- **Plants:** threads 2, 4, 7.
- **Cliffhanger:** it lets you pass, and the whole crowd leans to follow you, delighted. Hook for part 5: the road.

## Part 5: "ทางกลับบ้าน"

- **Hook:** จะเกิดอะไรขึ้น ถ้าคุณหนีออกมาได้ แต่ไม่มีใครไล่ตาม
- **Beats:** the forest edge; the head waits there, turns his clouded eyes exactly at you and says the last kind sentence (thread 4); you hurry on without answering; the road at night; headlights; home at dawn, relief; the memory returns (thread 8); the mirror (thread 6); the pattern on your shirt wears your face (thread 1); your pupils narrow (thread 2); your name is called and you turn late; at dusk the bell rings in a house with no bell and your head turns toward the window (thread 5). The change completes: you slip out of your human shape; you go back to the forest because that is where you are called. At the village edge **"you" and the head talk, both in snake form (user)**: he drops his human shape and says he knew from the first minute, that he was the first, that he chose to keep you in the game because you were the most fun; he asks, as he asked in part 1, "ตาบอดใช่มั้ย", and you answer. Behind them a new person wakes up on a mat in the village (the cycle starts again); the two snakes watch the new arrival. (call for the scene's details; the talk and "both in snake form" are the user's)
- **Close:** relief and danger together, no neat resolution; the snakes' pleasure is the final image; the loop (a new arrival) is the last picture.
