# "หมู่บ้านที่ทุกคนตาบอด": story plan for all five parts (for the user's approval)

Companion to `2026-10-23-blind-village-series-design.md` (premise, truth, rules, format). Written 2026-10-23. The user wants all parts planned together because every part links to the others. Marked **(user)** = decided by the user; **(call)** = the assistant's proposal, to be accepted or changed. The title "หมู่บ้านที่ทุกคนตาบอด" and part 3 are accepted by the user.

## Timeline (call)

Part 1: morning. Part 2: afternoon into dusk. Part 3: that night and the next day. Part 4: the next dawn to dusk, in the forest. Part 5: the night road, then home at dawn. Five days of fear compressed into about three.

## The threads that run through every part

Each thread is planted early, grows, and pays off in part 5.

1. **The shirt pattern (a snake with a human head) (user).** Part 1: everybody wears it; the face on the pattern is blank and calm. Part 2: it is on the cups, the well rim and the table cloth. Part 3: it is woven into the huts' mats and carved on the posts; "you" are given a shirt with the pattern to wear (call). Part 4: the same shape is carved on tree trunks, and the real snakes hang in that shape. Part 5: the face on your own shirt is your face (call).
2. **The eyes.** Villagers have clouded white eyes; "you" have pupils (the secret is visible to the viewer in every shot). Part 3: a test with something only a seeing person would avoid. Part 4: "you" must keep the eyes still while the snakes look. Part 5: your pupils narrow to a slit at the very end (call).
3. **The man who could see (user).** Part 2: the head tells his story (the foraging trip, he saw something, ran, vanished). Part 3: "you" find what he left (call): a shirt with no pattern hidden in a hut roof and a row of scratches on a post counting his days. Part 4: among the snakes in the trees "you" see one with a face that matches the face from that shirt's pocket photo or the scratches' maker (call): he did not vanish, he was turned. This sets the ending's rule before part 5.
4. **The head's two-sided sentences (user).** Part 1: "มีคนมาที่นี่อีกแล้วหรอ" and "ตาบอดใช่มั้ย". Part 2: "ถ้าคุณมองเห็น เราคงลำบากกันน่าดู". Part 3: he steers the villagers away from a test that would catch you, so he may know (call). Part 5: at the edge of the forest he turns his clouded eyes straight at "you" and says the last kind sentence; he knew from the first minute and lets "you" go (call).
5. **The evening bell (call).** Part 1: once at dusk a bell rings and every villager stops and turns the same way, toward the forest, for a few seconds. Part 2: the head says it is "to say thank you for the day". Part 3: "you" see what they face (a line of trees; something moves). Part 4: the snakes in the trees are what they were facing. Part 5: at home, at dusk, "you" hear the bell and your head turns by itself toward the window (the last sound and picture).
6. **No reflections, no names (call).** Part 1: no mirror, no still water; nobody asks for your name, they call you "คนใหม่". Part 3: a bowl of water is always covered. Part 5: at home the first mirror shows the change; someone calls your name and you do not turn at first.
7. **Care as a threat (user).** Part 2: the villagers say, with warmth, that they once made a seeing newcomer blind so that he would not vanish. Parts 3 and 4: every kind gesture is read twice by the viewer. Part 5: the head's kindness and the snakes' delight are the same gesture.
8. **Memory of arriving (user: "you" remember nothing).** Part 1: you wake with no memory. Part 3: a flash, a feeling of being carried through leaves. Part 5: the memory returns as a clear picture of snakes carrying you, laid down gently, and the cheering hiss of the trees as you finally leave the forest (the snakes are delighted with "you", user).

## Part 1: "ตื่นขึ้นมา"

- **Hook:** จะเกิดอะไรขึ้น ถ้าคุณตื่นมาในหมู่บ้านที่ทุกคนตาบอด แต่มีคุณคนเดียวที่มองเห็น
- **Beats:** wake with no memory (a woven mat, forest sounds); a figure walks up with a stick tapping, the head: "มีคนมาที่นี่อีกแล้วหรอ"; "คุณตาบอดใช่ไหม?" ; you answer "ใช่" (face-on close-up, the key beat); the walk through the village (huts, a well, a long table); everybody in the same pattern shirt; the pattern's calm blank face; no mirrors; the dusk bell and everyone turning toward the forest together (the first wrong thing); the head says "ยินดีต้อนรับ".
- **Plants:** threads 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 8.
- **Cliffhanger:** the villagers, still facing the forest, say together "ยินดีต้อนรับ" without turning to you, while the pattern on every shirt seems to look at you. Hook for part 2: the head promises to tell "you" why they are careful.

## Part 2: "คนที่มองเห็น"

- **Hook:** จะเกิดอะไรขึ้น ถ้าคนที่เคยมองเห็นในหมู่บ้านนี้หายไปแล้ว
- **Beats:** the communal table at dusk, the cups with the pattern; the head's story of the seeing man and the foraging trip (shown as a soft shadow-puppet like flashback so no gore); the line "ถ้าคุณมองเห็น เราคงลำบากกันน่าดู" (two meanings); the villagers mention, warmly, that they once made a seeing newcomer blind so that he would not vanish; the bell and the turn toward the forest.
- **Plants:** threads 3, 4, 5, 7.
- **Cliffhanger:** the head asks everyone to be glad that "you" cannot see; you realise what would happen if they found out. Hook for part 3: the first night.

## Part 3: "คืนแรก"

- **Hook:** จะเกิดอะไรขึ้น ถ้าคุณต้องแกล้งตาบอดต่อหน้าคนที่ไม่เคยหลับตา
- **Beats:** the hut at night; an old woman feeds you the same food as everyone and watches your hand; **the test:** a villager "accidentally" leaves a stool in your path, the head's stick moves it a second before you reach it (thread 4); next day, tasks (carrying water, weaving, with the pattern); you are given a pattern shirt; hidden in the roof you find the seeing man's patternless shirt, and scratches on a post (thread 3); a flash of being carried (thread 8); at the evening bell you see what moves in the line of trees (thread 5); you decide to leave.
- **Plants:** threads 1 to 8 all touched.
- **Cliffhanger:** the trees move again, in the shape of the pattern. Hook for part 4: you walk into the forest.

## Part 4: "ป่า"

- **Hook:** จะเกิดอะไรขึ้น ถ้าป่าที่คุณกำลังหนีเข้าไป มีอะไรมองคุณอยู่ทุกต้น
- **Beats:** dawn, you slip away; the forest is too quiet; foraging marks; you find a patternless shoe; the first snake with a human head above you, still; then a crowd on every branch, all turned toward you; one of them has the seeing man's face (thread 3); you recall the head's story and keep your eyes dead and your steps hesitant like a blind person; you walk under them (the long, silent crossing); one lowers its head to your face and looks straight into your eyes; you do not blink.
- **Plants:** threads 2, 3, 7.
- **Cliffhanger:** it lets you pass, and the whole crowd leans to follow you, delighted. Hook for part 5: the road.

## Part 5: "ทางกลับบ้าน"

- **Hook:** จะเกิดอะไรขึ้น ถ้าคุณหนีออกมาได้ แต่ไม่มีใครไล่ตาม
- **Beats:** the forest edge; the head waits there, turns his clouded eyes exactly at you, says the last kind sentence (thread 4); the road at night; headlights; home at dawn, relief; the memory returns (thread 8); the mirror (thread 6); the pattern on your shirt wears your face (thread 1); your pupils narrow (thread 2); your name is called and you turn late; at dusk the bell rings in a house with no bell and your head turns toward the window (thread 5). A last look at the trees outside: they are full of silent snakes, delighted.
- **Close:** relief and danger together, no neat resolution; the snakes' pleasure is the final image.
