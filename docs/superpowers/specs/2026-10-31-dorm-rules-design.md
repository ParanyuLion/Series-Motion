# Series 6 working title "กฎเหล็กหอพัก ตี 3" (design, 2026-10-31, for the user's approval)

The user's pitch (verbatim idea): at 3 a.m. a siren sounds and an ordinary dormitory becomes an execution ground; iron rules appear, "violators die at once"; the narrator's roommate dies horribly before his eyes; the narrator survives by luck; the horror has only begun because the rules change every hour.

## Why this works with what we know
Same DNA as the blind village hit: a closed place with a rule everybody obeys, one survivor who does not know the rule yet, a signal that repeats, and a truth told only at the end. New here: the rule is written down and changes, so the viewer plays along ("can you survive the next card?"), and the hours give a built-in clock and a built-in part structure.

## Format
- First person "ผม" (the user's pitch uses it; the BTS series did too). A standalone hook in every part ("เมื่อเสียงไซเรนดังตอนตี 3 หอพักธรรมดากลายเป็นลานประหาร กฎเหล็กบอกว่าผู้ฝ่าฝืนตายทันที และกฎจะเปลี่ยนใหม่ทุกชั่วโมง"), the title card names the series and the hour.
- Five parts of about 70 to 85 s, one part per hour: 03:00, 04:00, 05:00, 06:00, and the finale at dawn. Gemini Charon narration, burned-in Thai subtitles, 9:16, code-drawn like the other series, plus cartoonised photo backdrops.
- Deaths may be shown (user, 2026-10-31): the characters are cartoons, so the roommate's death is played on screen as a cartoon death, but with no blood at all (no red liquid, no wounds, no gore). The cruelty comes from how fast and how certain it is, a flash of red siren light and a hard cut, not from injury.

## The rule board (the motif) and the puzzle (user, 2026-10-31)
Each hour a new "iron rule" appears on a notice card on the corridor wall and through the PA. The card is a full-screen graphic with a siren sound and a red light sweep. The user wants the rules to feel brutal, as if there is no way to survive, and each part to be a puzzle game in which an unexpected way out exists. The design rules for every card:
1. The rule is written in absolute, cruel words, and on the obvious reading it cannot be obeyed (two rules that contradict, a rule that forbids everything, a rule that forbids doing and not doing).
2. The way out obeys the exact wording of the rule and uses something the viewer has already seen (a clue planted at least 10 s earlier), so the answer is surprising but fair.
3. The narrator finds it by paying attention, usually in the last third of the part, and the part ends on the cost (someone who did not find it dies) or on the next card.
4. The viewer is invited to guess: a short beat "คุณคิดออกมั้ย?" before the reveal, and the comments question is the riddle itself.
5. Examples to show the idea (the user and the assistant refine them part by part): "ห้ามอยู่ในห้อง และห้ามอยู่นอกห้อง" (the way out: stand in the doorway, on the threshold, which is neither); "ผู้พูดความจริงจะตาย ผู้พูดโกหกจะตาย" (the way out: say "ผมจะตายเพราะประโยคนี้", a sentence the rule cannot judge, or stay silent if silence is not forbidden); "ห้ามอยู่ใกล้ใคร และห้ามอยู่คนเดียว" (the way out: stand next to something that counts as a person in the rule's own words, such as the cleaner's sign or a mirror).

## Cast (placeholders, the user may rename)
- ผม: a first-year student, the narrator (the turnable traveller model with glasses and a student ID lanyard).
- เพื่อนร่วมห้อง (ต้น): dies in the hook of Part 1, by breaking the first rule in front of the narrator (implied).
- ป้าแม่บ้าน (in truth the night nurse): mops the corridor and seems to know every rule before it appears; never explains.
- พี่ปี 4: has survived since the first siren and has a notebook of rule cards; helps and withholds in turn.
- A girl from the third floor who obeys every card a second too late.

## Parts
1. **03:00 "กฎข้อแรก"**: the siren, rule 1 (do not turn on any light), the roommate switches on the lamp and dies (a cartoon death, no blood). The narrator survives only by luck (his phone was dead). He meets the cleaner in the dark corridor. The puzzle here is small: the first card, a clue that the lights are the trigger (the phone's dead screen, the lamp's cord).
2. **04:00 "กฎที่ขัดกัน"**: rule 2 (an example: "ห้ามอยู่ในห้อง และห้ามอยู่นอกห้อง") looks impossible; he and the older student work it out (the doorway). In the corridor he sees the older student's notebook of cards from earlier nights.
3. **05:00 "ห้ามไว้ใจกฎ"**: rule 3 is a truth-and-lie trap (an example: the liar paradox said aloud). The older student gives him a card that is not on the board. The third-floor girl dies by answering the way the old card told her to.
4. **06:00 "กฎของคนที่รอด"**: rule 4 (an example: "ห้ามอยู่ใกล้ใคร และห้ามอยู่คนเดียว") is the first rule that needs another person: the narrator must choose to go back for someone, and the way out only works if he does.
5. **Finale "กฎข้อสุดท้าย"**: the truth; the siren, the rules and the cleaner explained; the loop ending (see below).

## The truth (decided with the user, 2026-10-31: waking on a hospital bed, in the spirit of Alice in Borderland)
The dorm is not real. The narrator and everyone else in the building were caught in a fire (the first siren was the fire alarm) and are lying in a hospital between life and death; the dorm is a place in between, and the hourly "iron rules" are survival games. "Violators die at once" is a patient crossing over (a flatline). The cleaner is the night nurse who keeps watch on them; she cannot say so, she can only hand out cards. The final rule that lets a player wake up is the one that cannot be solved alone: do not leave anyone behind (the narrator goes back for someone). Finale: he wakes on a hospital bed, a drip in his arm, a wristband with his name, a heart monitor beeping at the rhythm of the siren. The nurse is the cleaner. The roommate ต้น's bed is empty (the ending note below). This is inspired by the premise of that show only; the games, the rules, the cast and the clues are our own.
Planted clues across the five parts (so the twist is fair): the siren and the heart monitor share one rhythm; the narrator's student lanyard looks like a hospital wristband in close-ups; the ceiling is a white tiled ceiling with a flat light; the floor tiles are hospital linoleum; a drip stand stands in the corridor corner; the cleaner's trolley carries a medicine tray under the cloths; every part has a small heart-monitor line in a corner of the card frames, going flat at a death.
Ending (the assistant's call, the user may overrule): he wakes up; the nurse tells him he was out for hours and that "the others in your room did not wake up"; at the bedside is a rule card in the nurse's handwriting: "ห้ามลืมว่าใครที่คุณทิ้งไว้ข้างหลัง"; the monitor beeps once more at 03:00 and the last shot is the next bed, where a new patient's eyes open.

## Look
A cheap old Thai university dormitory: long corridor, numbered doors, fluorescent tubes, a red emergency light, wet tile floors, dark stairwells. Backdrops from CC0 or public-domain photos (a dormitory corridor, a stairwell, a bedroom) cartoonised with `scripts/shadow1/cartoon.py`, a cold grade with a red siren sweep. Rule cards drawn in code in a stern Thai notice-board style.
