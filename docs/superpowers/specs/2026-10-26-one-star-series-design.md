## The rule

He takes the LATEST person who gave him 1 star: he hunts them until they ride with him to "ที่หมาย"; arriving means they are gone and the next night they are the pale driver. The "latest" can change: if another person gives him 1 star after you, the hunt moves to that person (the loophole you find in Part 4).
- He never runs or shouts; he walks and drives slowly and always arrives. The fear is inevitability.
- The app is the instrument: a message "คนขับกำลังมารับคุณ ถึงใน 12 นาที" with a countdown that cannot be deleted; it appears again after the app is closed or the phone is off; the chime gets slower each time.

- Each night he is paler; the corpse face.
